Courses at Chitkara university

Chitkara University offers a range of courses to help students build strong subject knowledge and practical skills. Students learn through classes, projects, assignments, laboratory work, and other learning activities.

Computer Science

This course introduces students to programming, computer systems, databases, web development, and problem-solving. Students practise writing code and learn how technology is used to create useful applications.

Business Studies

Students learn about business management, marketing, accounting, entrepreneurship, and how organisations operate. Activities and projects help them understand business ideas and decision-making.

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.)

The Bachelor of Science program helps students build a strong foundation in scientific subjects. Students study concepts through classroom lessons, practical laboratory work, projects, and assignments. The program develops analytical thinking, observation, and problem-solving skills, preparing students for further study or careers in science and related fields.

Learning and Support

Faculty members guide students throughout their courses. The university also encourages group work, independent study, and participation in academic activities.
