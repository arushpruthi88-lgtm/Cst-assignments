Our Faculty

The faculty at Chitkara University is made up of experienced teachers and subject specialists who are committed to helping students learn and grow. They explain concepts clearly, encourage questions, and guide students throughout their studies.

Teaching and Learning

Faculty members use lectures, discussions, assignments, projects, and practical activities to make learning engaging. They help students connect what they learn in class with real-world situations.

Student Support

Teachers provide academic guidance, answer students’ questions, and offer feedback on their work. Students can approach faculty members for help with coursework, projects, and planning their studies.
