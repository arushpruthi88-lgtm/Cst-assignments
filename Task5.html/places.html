Famous Places in India

Taj Mahal, Agra

The Taj Mahal is a famous white marble monument in Agra and a symbol of love.

India Gate, Delhi

India Gate is a war memorial in New Delhi, built to honour Indian soldiers.

Qutub Minar, Delhi

Qutub Minar is a historic tower in Delhi and one of India's best-known monuments.

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

Hawa Mahal is a pink palace in Jaipur, known for its many small windows.

Golden Temple, Amritsar

The Golden Temple is a peaceful and sacred place in Amritsar, Punjab.
